## 1. Corrections Made (Audit Disclosures)

The original Stage 1 gate report claimed a clean PASS, but Stage R revealed multiple severe integrity violations and unbacked claims:

1. **Synthetic Legal Corpus Deleted and Replaced with 30 Real Official PDFs (R1):**
   - *What was wrong:* The original 28 PDFs in `data/raw/legal/` (`LEGAL-001` through `LEGAL-028`) were not real downloads. They were synthetic 2-page documents generated locally via ReportLab using synthesized paraphrases.
   - *Correction:* In R1.1 and R1.2 (commits `1aea5af`, `86598d7`), audited and permanently deleted all 28 generated PDFs. In R1.3 (commit `f10b668`), downloaded **30 real, unmodified original PDFs** (~24.5 MB) directly from official portals (`prsindia.org`, `ielrc.org`, `tnrd.tn.gov.in`, `cms.tn.gov.in`, `cwc.gov.in`, `moef.gov.in`, `cgwb.gov.in`). In R1.4 (commit `eafca2e`), rebuilt `data/raw/legal/manifest.json` (`v2.0.0`) with SHA-256 digests, page counts, and extraction methods (`text_layer` vs `ocr_needed`). In R1.5 (commit `82f5552`), replaced tests with strict integrity checks preventing any banned synthetic hash. In R1.6 (commit `05d8139`), manually spot-checked 5 documents (`LEGAL-001`, `LEGAL-003`, `LEGAL-012`, `LEGAL-017`, `LEGAL-026`).
2. **Parametric Tank Polygons Replaced with Real OSM / Traced Geometries (R2):**
   - *What was wrong:* In Step 1.3, 14 "derived" tanks used synthetic crescent-shaped parametric polygons generated by `_build_crescent_tank_polygon()`. Additionally, tank health labels (`healthy`, `seasonal`, `shrinking`) were assigned without measurement.
   - *Correction:* In R2.1 (commit `ebe822e`), deleted `_build_crescent_tank_polygon()`. Extracted 21 tank polygons directly from OpenStreetMap via the official OSM REST API v0.6, and traced 14 tank polygons from real Sentinel-2 L2A post-monsoon GeoTIFFs (`NDWI >= 0.01`). In R2.2 (commit `9b64979`), reset all health labels to `"unverified"` until measured in Stage 2. In R2.3 (commit `5721c11`), generated Sentinel-2 True Color RGB + NDWI overlay PNGs for all tanks (`docs/data/overlays/*.png`). One tank (`TN-KPM-013`, Nathapettai Eri) failed the overlay check with only 9.0% open water due to NH48 bisection and heavy weed infestation. In R2.4 (commit `8a09573`), dropped `TN-KPM-013`, leaving **35 verified pilot tanks** (all 35 passing the overlay check, exceeding the 30-tank minimum).
3. **Feasibility Spike Rewritten to Record Real Payload Content (R3):**
   - *What was wrong:* The original feasibility spike recorded HTTP `200 OK` status codes as evidence without recording data content, values, dates, or distances.
   - *Correction:* In R3.1 and R3.2 (commits `4458643`, `d3eb90c`), rewrote `feasibility_spike.py` to capture actual payload data: 2024 STAC scene counts and cloud cover, JRC GSW pixel values sampled at tank centroids via `rasterio`, NASA POWER 15-day daily precipitation time series, 25 unique India-WRIS CGWB groundwater piezometer readings with geodesic distances to each tank, and OSM v0.6 vertex counts and geodesic areas. Saved to `data/raw/samples/feasibility_spike.json`. In R3.3 (commit `145d1dc`), segregated live network calls under `@pytest.mark.network` and `make test-network`.
4. **Monsoon Cloud Cover Risk Quantified (R4):**
   - *What was wrong:* Risk `R-003` was noted in passing as a medium risk without multi-year quantitative backing.
   - *Correction:* In R4.1 (commit `e86655e`), queried STAC for all Sentinel-2 L2A scenes from 2019 to 2026 across all 35 pilot tanks (`3,150` tank-months, `27,330` deduplicated tank-scene observations), saved to `data/processed/cloud_stats.parquet`, and plotted `docs/data/cloud_cover_seasonality.png`. Proved that during Northeast Monsoon (`Oct–Dec`), only `18.27%` of passes have `< 20%` cloud cover (`1.62` passes/tank/month, dropping to `12%` in Nov) and `35.58%` have `< 40%` cloud cover (`3.15` passes/mo), compared to `43.40%` in Dry Season (`Jan–May`). In R4.2 (commit `75cc317`), updated `PROGRESS.md` and authored `docs/adr/0003-cloud-cover-and-forecast-design.md` detailing Stage 2 `SCL` pixel-level gating, Stage 5 irregular-cadence features + continuous rainfall covariates, and Stage 9 n8n cloudy-pass fallback.
