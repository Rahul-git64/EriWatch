2. In the letter first read above, the Director of Rural Development and Panchayat
Raj has sent a proposai stating that Tamil Nadu is entirely depending on monsoon rains
for recharging its water resources. The failure of monsoon and increasing usage of
water for irrigation, domestic and industrial purposes has led to an over exploitation of
ground water leading to reduction in ground water potential which is evident from the
fact that out of 385 blocks, 165 blocks are over exploited and critical in terms of water
.stress in the State.
3. The Director of Rural Development and Panchayat Raj has further stated that in
rejuvenation of Minor Irrigation (Ml) Tanks, Ponds and Ooranies vested with Panchayat
Union and Village Panchayat. There are about 21,609 Minor Irrigation (Ml) Tanks

4. The Director of Rural Development and Panchayat Raj has further stated that
Government has clearly spelt out its priority on the rejuvenation of Minor Irrigation (Ml)
tanks, ponds and Ooranies in rural areas by launching the concept of Kudimaramathu.
Based on the concept of Kudimaramathu Rural Development and Panchayat Raj
Department has proposed to take up the restoration of Ml tanks/Ponds and Ooranies
through implementation of Kudimaramathu. It has been proposed to take up the
desilting of Ponds and Ooranies through the use of machineries by the concerned
Village Panchayat and the desilting of Ml tanks will be executed through the use of
machineries by the concerned Panchayat Union by adopting the Public Works
Department (PWD) Schedule of Rates (SoR) for the year 2019-20, under the State

weir, sluice for Ml Tank and construction of baby pond, bathing ghat, inlet and outlet
structures for Ponds and Ooranies will be taken up at a cost of Rs.750 Crore under
convergence with Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme

5. The Director of Rural Development and Panchayat Raj has sent the draft
guidelines for the Improvements to Minor lrrigation Tanks, Ponds and Ooranies during
2019-20for approval.
6. The Director of Rural Development and Panchayat Raj has therefore requested
the Government to approve the draft guidelines and also requested to accord
permission for (i) taking up the desilting and deepening of 5,ooo Ml tanks through
machineries at an estimate cost of Rs.5o0·Crore from the State Funds by adopting
PWD SoR for the year 2019-20 and (i) taking up the construction of other works in Ml
Tanks/Ponds/Ooranies like inlet, outlet, surplus weir, sluice, baby pond, bathing ghat at
an estimate cost of Rs.750 Crore under convergence with the Mahatma Gandhi
National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme funds.
7. The Government, after careful examination of the proposal of the Director of
Rural Development and Panchayat Raj, have decided to accept the proposal and
accord sanction for the following:
1.
taking up the desilting and deepening of 5,ooo Ml tanks through
Panchayat Unions and 25,0o0 Ponds and Ooranies through Village
Panchayats using machineries at an estimate cost of Rs.5o0 Crore from
the State Funds by adopting PWD SoR for the year 2019-20 and
ii.
taking up the construction of other works in Ml Tanks/Ponds/Ooranies like
inlet, outlet, surplus weir, sluice, baby pond, bathing ghat at an estimate
cost of Rs.750 Crore under convergence with the Mahatma Gandhi
National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme funds.
